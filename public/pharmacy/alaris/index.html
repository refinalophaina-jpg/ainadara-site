    Reference & simulation boundaries

    The generic control layout is based on the 8015 front panel. The public v12.1 manual is our workflow reference, not a verified match to your pump’s firmware. A library-update date does not establish that version.

    Practice startup, profile selection, drug/fluid browsing, keypad programming and independent primary infusions on channels A–D. This trainer adds a review checkpoint and override annotation. Weight setup, menus and alarm recovery are simplified; arrow-key increments follow the entered decimal precision.

    Optional synthesized training tones are not manufacturer alarm signals and have no clinical meaning. Exact alarm/silence timing, channel power-off, simultaneous-alert priority, secondary infusion, bolus, KVO, PCA and syringe workflows are not simulated. Demo values are fictional; local imports are not clinically validated.

    Import format & prototype boundaries

    Import the normalized CSV template, JSON, or a SharePoint Drug Library CSV export. The SharePoint reader preserves all source records and identifies a programmable subset. IQY connections, field markup and source links are never executed.

    Fixed-concentration primary pump infusions and fluids are supported. Intermittent, anesthesia, PCA, syringe-only, variable-concentration, ambiguous and inconsistent entries remain browse-only. Therapy variants and original preparations remain separate. Any unit conversion is explicit in Source details.

    Blank export limits remain unknown—not zero or confirmed disabled. Only supplied limits are checked. Modification timestamps are not an approved library effective date. Advisory strings may be labels or codes rather than full policy text.

    Manual imports stay in browser-tab memory; nothing is uploaded. The private local preview may load a local snapshot from its loopback server. Guided exercises use fictional values. The public site package never contains the workplace library.
